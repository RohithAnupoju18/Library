Library Rules & Regulations

Borrowing Rules

  • Students may borrow up to 5 books at a time for a period of 14 days.
  • Faculty members can borrow up to 10 books depending on the subject area and department.
  • Books are renewed only if no other user has requested them.
  • Reference materials and journals are to be used within the library premises.
  • Members must present their library ID card while checking out or renewing materials.

General Conduct

  • Maintain silence in reading zones and study halls to support a focused environment.
  • Use mobile phones in silent mode and avoid calls inside the reading and reference areas.
  • Do not mark, damage, or remove pages from any library resource.
  • Food and beverages are not allowed in the reading and stacks areas.
  • Any loss or damage of books must be reported immediately and may incur replacement charges.
